# Lysogeny broth (LB)

Used as a classic and easy rich/complex media to cultivate E. coli.
[Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lysogeny_broth).

??? question "Pouring plates?"

    You can prepare this as a "broth" or as "agar" petri plates.
    It's the same, except that for plates you add agar just before autoclaving
    and then have to pour the plates (ofc).

    See [pouring agar plates](pouring-agar-plates.md) for more.

1. Decide how much you want/need. Calculate how much of each reagent you need
    to be adding:

    | What     | g/L |
    | :------- | :----------------------------------- |
    | peptone       | 10 |
    | yeast extract | 5 |
    | NaCl          | 5 |

1. Get an appropriate[^app-container] clean glass container autoclave it in.
    Get a clean graduated cylinder that can hold the total amount you want to
    make.

[^app-container]: This means a clean glass container with a capacity of about 2x to 1.5x volume of the amount you want to autoclave, but still fits in whatever autoclave you're using. Check that. You may be able to split one batch or prepare multiple batches if need be.

1. Put approximately 80% of the water you'll need into your 
    flask/bottle/container that you're going to autoclave it in.

1. Weigh out the components (as you caculated above) and add them.
    Here I mean peptone, yeast extract, and NaCl.
    I recommend in that order, but feel free to try other ways.

1. Swirl to mix it. You don't need a stir bar. If it's not completely 
    dissolved, remember that you're not[^not-physio] doing 
    physiology studies with this.

1. Pour it into the clean graduated cylinder.
    Rinse the autoclavable container you used to mix it, and use that to
    bring the volume up to the volume you want.

    Discard any extra rinsate water that would have brought it over volume.

1. Pour it back into the empty autoclaveable container you used to mix 
    everything in. Cap it appropriately for autoclaving.

1. Autoclave it.

1. When it's done, close the cap or pour the plates, as appropriate.

[^not-physio]: If you are, you should be using other media.
