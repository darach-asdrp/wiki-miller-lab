# YPD broth media

## Background

The goal of a cell is to make two cells. This is true of yeast. In order to do
this, they need the raw materials.

## Important points

Do not autoclave the media
with the glucose mixed in with the yeast extract / peptone, as it will
carmelize and inhibit the growth of the yeast.

## Supplies 

### Gear

- Container to autoclave it in
    - Recommend a screw-top bottle (with plastic pour ring installed) with 
    twice the volume as media you intend to autoclave, washed and rinsed 
    well with at least RO/DI water
- Graduated cylinder sufficient to measure full volume of media
- Autoclave

### Reagents

- Peptone
- Yeast extract
    - Different brands of yeast extract apparently can be clear or cloudy when
    you prepare the media, so account for type of yeast extract in YPD when
    using for a spectroscopic (OD 600) measurement of the media
- sterile 20% glucose
    You can use higher percentage of glucose, for example 40%, 
    but that's a bit harder to get into solution than 20%

## Procedure

## Outcome

You should now have YPD broth media.
It should be a clear but strongly brown/orange color.

If you did not add antibiotics or other potentially-temperature-sensitive
compounds to the media, you should store it at room-temperature.
This allows potentially growing contaminants a chance to grow and be noticed
by you before you try and use the media yourself!
