Ecoli electroporation, RT - v0.2 

1. Grow up whatever ecoli strain you want to transform. 

    You probably[^alternmatives] want to innoculate it in 2-4mL LB and 
    incubate at either 37C for 16 hours or 30C for 24 hours.

[^alternmatives]: I believe you can just back it from old media too, or 
scrape cells from a reasonably fresh agar plate, but a fresh liquid culture 
is much much more controllable.

1. In morning, back-dilute/innoculate cells to grow again at 37C shaking in LB.

    Barrick suggests adding 100uL of a saturated overnight culture onto 10mL 
    LB, or ~0.05 OD. I recommend you do something similar, or ~200uL onto 20mL in
    a baffled shake flask.

1. Incubate cells until they're at ~0.6OD, so for ~2-3 hours for above 
liquid recommendation. But, doing this at ~1.0 OD also works.

1. Centrifuge cells ~3krcf at RT to pellet 

1. Dump super and aspirated all super, R/S with 1mL water (RT) and move to 
sterile eppendorf tube.

1. Spin again, aspirate all super and R/S with 1mL water

1. Spin again, aspirate all super and R/S with 1mL water 

1. Spin again, aspirate all super and R/S with a small volume (see below) of 
water (RT, and clean no salts)

1. Combine 30uL of cells with ~1uL of clean DNA and put into a 1mm-gap cuvette

1. Electroporate at 1.5kV, 25uF, time constants should be about 4.5-5.5ms

1. Immediately take cuvette to BSCabinet and add 1mL of fresh LB or SOC media.
    Immediately flush this through with a pipette to resuspend the cells from 
    the cuvette gap. Pick up as much of this as you can and put into a sterile
    eppendorf.

1. For Ampicillin/Carbenicillin selection you can plate immediately but for
other markers you need to let these rest at 37C for about an hour to recover.

1. Then plate it on a selective plate. It's up to you if you want to plate
    100uL volume on a whole plate using 5-8 glass beads (sterile obvs), or
    if you want to spot it onto a sector and streak, or whatever.
